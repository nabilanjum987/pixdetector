About PixDetector

The world's most comprehensive free AI image detection platform

Our Mission

PixDetector was built with one mission: to make powerful AI image detection tools accessible to everyone, for free. In a world where AI-generated images and deepfakes are becoming increasingly common, we believe everyone should have access to tools that help them verify what's real.

From journalists verifying news photos to individuals checking social media images, PixDetector provides professional-grade AI analysis without any cost or signup.
