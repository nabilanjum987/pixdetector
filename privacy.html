Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Your Privacy Is Our Priority

At PixDetector, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our free AI image analysis tools.

Images You Upload

We never store your images. When you upload an image for analysis, it is sent directly to our AI analysis service, processed in real-time, and immediately discarded. No images are saved to our servers, databases, or any third-party storage.

Your images are never used for training AI models, never shared with third parties, and never accessible to any human reviewer at PixDetector.

Data We Collect

We collect minimal, anonymized data to improve our service:

  • Anonymous usage statistics (which tools are used, how often)
  • Basic technical information (browser type, country — no IP stored)
  • Error logs to improve reliability (no personal data included)

We do NOT collect: your name, email, images, image content, or any personally identifiable information.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies for basic website functionality and anonymous analytics (Google Analytics). No advertising cookies are used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Third Party Services

We use Anthropic's Claude AI API for image analysis. Images sent for analysis are subject to Anthropic's data handling policies. Anthropic does not store or train on API inputs by default.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at privacy@pixdetector.com
