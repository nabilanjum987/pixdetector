What is an AI Generated Image Checker?

An AI generated image checker is a tool that uses artificial intelligence to analyze images and determine whether they were created by AI image generators or captured by a real camera. With the rise of tools like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, and Stable Diffusion, AI-generated images have become nearly indistinguishable from real photographs.

PixDetector's AI image checker analyzes dozens of visual signals — including pixel patterns, texture consistency, lighting physics, and structural anomalies — to accurately determine the origin of any image.

How Our AI Image Checker Works

Our tool uses advanced machine learning models trained on millions of real and AI-generated images. When you upload an image, the AI analyzes it across multiple dimensions: texture patterns (AI generators create distinctive pixel signatures), lighting consistency (real photos follow physics, AI often doesn't), structural anomalies (AI frequently fails with hands, text, and edges), and frequency domain analysis (different statistical properties between real and AI images).

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI generated image checker?

Our AI image checker achieves up to 94% accuracy on standard benchmarks. Accuracy may vary based on the AI generator used, post-processing applied, and image compression. New AI models may initially have slightly lower detection rates as our models continuously update.

Which AI image generators can be detected?

Our checker can detect images from Midjourney (v4, v5, v6), DALL-E 2 and 3, Stable Diffusion (all versions), Adobe Firefly, Google Imagen, Bing Image Creator, and most other major AI image generators available today.

Is my image stored after checking?

No. Your privacy is our top priority. Images are analyzed in real-time and immediately discarded. We do not store, log, or share any images uploaded to PixDetector.

Can the checker be fooled by edited AI images?

Images that have been heavily post-processed, compressed multiple times (e.g., screenshotted and re-uploaded), or edited extensively in Photoshop may have lower detection accuracy. However, our models are trained to handle many such scenarios.

Is the AI Generated Image Checker really free?

Yes, completely free. No signup, no credit card, no limits. PixDetector is committed to providing free AI detection tools for everyone.

What file formats are supported?

We support JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, and GIF formats. For best results, upload the original image without additional compression. Maximum file size is 10MB.
