About This Tool

Image verification is the process of confirming whether a photograph is genuine or has been digitally altered. This tool is built around a professional workflow — the kind of check a journalist or legal team runs before an image goes into a story or a case file, not a casual curiosity check.

How It Works

The AI model examines the image for signs commonly associated with digital manipulation or synthetic generation: inconsistent lighting and shadows, unnatural edges around objects, repeating or overly smooth pixel patterns, and other artifacts that tend to survive even after an image is re-saved or lightly edited. It weighs these signals together into a probability and a plain verdict, along with a written explanation of what drove that conclusion.

What the Results Mean

Treat the verdict as a starting point for editorial judgment, not a final ruling — especially at "Uncertain" or "Suspicious," which mean the model found mixed or ambiguous signals rather than a clean answer either way. For anything going into publication or a legal record, pair this result with a source check (who sent it, where it was originally posted) rather than relying on the tool alone.

Limitations

No automated tool can serve as sole proof of authenticity in a legal or journalistic context. A "Real Photo" verdict means the model found no red flags, not a guarantee of provenance — it doesn't know where the image came from or who took it.

FAQ

Can this be used as legal evidence?

No — treat it as an investigative aid, not proof. Courts require chain-of-custody and forensic documentation this tool doesn't provide.

What's the difference from the Authenticity Checker?

This tool is built around a professional pre-publication workflow with a written explanation. The Authenticity Checker gives a quicker genuine-vs-manipulated read for everyday use.

Does it detect AI-generated images too?

Yes — the same signal analysis flags both AI-generated images and manually manipulated real photos.
