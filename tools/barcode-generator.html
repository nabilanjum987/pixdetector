About This Tool

Barcodes are how the physical world gets a machine-readable name. A warehouse scans a Code 128 label to find a box, a shop scans an EAN-13 at the till, a hospital scans a Pharmacode to verify packaging. This generator creates those same standard barcodes right in your browser — nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no watermark or usage limit. Type your data, pick the format that matches where the barcode will be used, and download it as a PNG for documents or an SVG for professional printing.

Every format follows its official specification, including check digits. For EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8 and ITF-14, type the number without its final digit and the check digit is calculated and added automatically; type the full number and it is verified instead. The batch mode generates a whole sheet at once — one value per line, up to 40 — so labelling a shelf or a stock delivery takes minutes, not hours.

FAQ

Is this barcode generator really free?

Yes. Create and download unlimited barcodes as PNG or SVG — no watermark, no sign-up, no usage limit. The generator runs entirely in your browser.

Will barcodes from here scan properly?

Yes. The output follows the official specification for each format, including check digits, so any scanner or phone app can read it. For reliable scanning keep dark bars on a light background and don't crop the white margin around the code.

Can I put an EAN-13 or UPC from here on a product I sell in shops?

The codes themselves are technically valid, but retailers require product numbers that are registered with GS1 in your country, so your number is uniquely yours. Use this generator freely for internal labels, inventory, assets and print tests — and register with GS1 before printing numbers on products for retail sale.

Should I download PNG or SVG?

SVG for printing and design work — it stays perfectly sharp at any size. PNG for documents, spreadsheets and websites; PNGs here are exported at 3× size, so they print cleanly as well.

Do I need to work out the check digit myself?

No. For EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8 and ITF-14, type the number without the final digit and the check digit is calculated and added automatically. If you type the full number including the check digit, it gets verified instead — and flagged if it's wrong.
