The problem: countdowns that need an account, or show the wrong time

People looking for a countdown timer usually want one of two simple things: "how long until my birthday / exam / holiday / launch" or "give me 25 minutes and ring when it ends". What they keep running into is friction. Many countdown sites ask you to create an account, show ads around the timer, or only work as an embed code for a website. Worse, shared countdowns often show the wrong time — a timer set in one time zone reads differently in another, and image-based countdowns in emails restart from the beginning every time the message is opened. And the classic complaint about browser timers: start one, switch to another tab, and it rings minutes late because the browser throttled it.

How do I start a free countdown timer?

Pick a duration — type hours, minutes and seconds or tap a preset like 5, 25 or 60 minutes — and press Start, or switch to the Date tab and pick the date and time you are counting down to. The countdown runs instantly in your browser, free, with no signup and no ads. It is measured against your device clock, so it stays accurate even when you switch tabs, and the remaining time shows in the tab title so you can watch it without coming back to the page.

A countdown timer is a tool that shows the time remaining until a chosen moment — a duration from now, or a calendar date and time — counting down second by second to zero and then signalling that the time is up. A duration countdown answers "how much time is left in this session", while a date countdown answers "how many days until the event". Both need the same thing to be trusted: an accurate clock, a visible display, and an alarm you can actually hear.

How to use this countdown timer in 3 steps

Step 1: choose a duration or a date — type a time, tap a preset, or pick a calendar date and time. Step 2: press Start. The big display counts down live; press Fullscreen for a classroom, gym or presentation display. Step 3: when it reaches zero, a beep plays and the tab title changes. Press Stop alarm to silence it, or Reset to run the same countdown again.

Why this countdown stays accurate in background tabs

Many online timers count ticks — they subtract one second per timer call. Browsers slow those calls down in hidden tabs to save battery, so a tick-counting timer runs slow the moment you look away. This one works from a deadline instead: pressing Start records the exact moment the countdown should end against your device clock, and every reading is simply the difference between that moment and now. A hidden tab, a throttled page, or a laptop that slept and woke does not change the answer — the alarm fires when it should, and the tab title carries the time remaining.

FAQ

Is this countdown timer really free?

Yes. Set as many countdowns as you like, free, with no signup, no ads and no download. Everything runs in your browser.

How do I count down to a specific date?

Switch to the Date tab, pick your date and time — a birthday, exam, holiday or launch — and press Start. The page shows the days, hours, minutes and seconds left, live, in your own time zone.

Will the countdown keep running if I switch tabs?

Yes. The countdown is measured against your device clock, not by counting ticks, so it stays accurate in a background tab and the remaining time shows in the tab title. Keep the tab open — closing it stops the countdown.

What happens when the countdown reaches zero?

The display shows 00:00, a beep alarm plays, and the tab title changes to say the countdown is done. Press Stop alarm to silence it, or Reset to run it again.

Can I share my countdown with someone?

You can share this page link, but the countdown itself is not saved or synced — the other person would need to set the same date on their own device. For a countdown that must be identical for everyone, name the date and time zone in your message.

Why do some countdown timers show the wrong time?

Most wrong countdowns are time-zone mistakes: the timer was set in one time zone and read in another, or a shared image countdown restarted from the beginning on every view. This tool counts down in your own time zone, so the number you see is the time left for you.
