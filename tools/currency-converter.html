The problem: every converter shows a different number, and none of them says when

Ask three currency converters what 100 dollars is in euros and you will get three different answers — and most of them will not tell you why. One is using this morning's rate, one is using yesterday's, and one has quietly baked in a margin without saying so. The travel forums are full of the same story: someone budgeted a trip on a converter's number, then found the hotel cost 10% more because the bank's rate, the card's fee and the weekend's stale rate all piled up. The worst version of this is a converter that fails silently — the rate feed goes down and the page keeps showing an old number as if it were live, or quietly substitutes 1.0 and tells you 100 dollars equals 100 euros. What people want is not another number. They want a number they can trust: the rate, the date of the rate, the source of the rate, and an honest admission when the rate is not available.

Mid-market, bank rate, and what you actually pay

The mid-market rate is the wholesale price large banks trade at with each other — the fair benchmark, and the rate this page shows. Your bank, card network or transfer service adds a spread on top, typically 0.5% to 4%, plus sometimes a fixed fee. So if this page says 100 USD = 89.34 EUR and your bank offers 87.50 EUR, the bank kept about 2% — that is normal, and now you can measure it instead of guessing. Airport and hotel counters are the worst deal of all, often 5–10% below mid-market.

What this converter cannot do

It cannot tell you what your bank will charge — only your provider can. It cannot convert cryptocurrency, and it cannot give you a real-time ticking rate: the ECB reference rate updates once a day, so intraday moves are not reflected. On weekends the rate shown is Friday's closing rate, because the foreign-exchange market is closed. It also cannot fetch fresh rates without a connection: if the feed is down, the page falls back to the last rates it saved in your browser, with the age stamped on them — so you can still convert offline, but you can see exactly how old the numbers are. All of that is stated on the result itself, next to the date.

FAQ

How do I convert USD to EUR?

Enter the amount in USD, select USD as From and EUR as To, and press Convert. The tool multiplies by the current USD/EUR rate and shows the converted amount plus the rate used and its date.

What is the mid-market rate?

The midpoint between the wholesale buy and sell prices of a currency, the fairest benchmark there is. Banks and card networks add their own margin on top, so what you receive is usually a little less. This page shows the mid-market rate.

Why is the rate at my bank / the airport different from Google?

Google shows the mid-market rate; your bank adds a margin, typically 1–4% worse, and calls the worse figure its exchange rate. Airport kiosks are the most expensive, at 5–12% below mid-market, because their customers are captive and last-minute.

Why does every currency converter show a different price?

Different sources, different update times, and different margins. Some show clean mid-market, some bake in a spread, some use a daily snapshot. Compare against a named mid-market source and check the update timestamp, which this page shows on every result.

How often do exchange rates update?

Continuously during market hours, as foreign exchange runs 24 hours a day, 5 days a week. This page's source, the Frankfurter API, republishes the European Central Bank reference rate once a day, so intraday moves are not reflected and weekend rates are Friday's closing rate.

Can I use a currency converter offline?

A web page cannot fetch fresh rates offline. This page saves the last rates in your browser, so if the feed is down it keeps converting from the cached rates and stamps their age on the result, so you can see how old the numbers are.

Which is the best free currency converter?

It depends on the job: quick lookups, Google Finance; transfers, Wise, which shows the real mid-market rate with a transparent fee; offline travel, XE; reporting, the ECB. No single converter is best for every use.

Is XE or Wise better?

For converting and looking up, both are trusted. For sending money, Wise shows the real mid-market rate with a transparent fee, while Xe's transfer rates may include a small margin, with fees waived above $1,000. In one Finder comparison from September 2026, Wise delivered more rupees on both $500 and $10,000 test transfers to India.

What is the difference between an exchange rate and a conversion fee?

The rate is the underlying price of the currency; the fee is the service charge, flat, percentage, or hidden as a markup inside a worse rate. This page's markup calculator shows the hidden part in money terms.

Do I lose money converting currency twice?

Yes, in practice. Each conversion pays a spread and possibly a fee, and rounding to the smallest unit shaves a little more. Converting there and back at the same mid-market rate still loses money to the two spreads.

Should I pay in local currency or my home currency abroad?

Local currency. Decline dynamic currency conversion at the till or ATM: the shop's rate is usually worse than your card network's.

How do I calculate a conversion by hand?

Multiply the amount by the exchange rate, for example 100 USD × 0.8902 = 89.02 EUR. For two non-dollar currencies, convert via USD as the cross rate.

Are converter rates the same as bank rates?

Not usually. Currency converters typically display the mid-market rate, while banks and exchange services generally add a markup or spread. Use this page to check the benchmark, then compare it with the quote you are offered.

Can I convert currency for travel budgeting?

Yes, that is the main use case: vacation budgeting, invoice conversion and online shopping price checks. The rate is an estimate for planning, not a locked quote.

What happens if the rate can't be loaded?

This page shows the last cached rates with their age stamped, so you can still convert, and a Refresh rates button to retry the live feed. If no cached rates exist yet for that pair, it shows a clear error instead of a made-up number.
