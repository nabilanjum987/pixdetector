The problem: an HTML file that needs to be a PDF, right now

It is a small, recurring job: an invoice saved as HTML, a report a colleague sent as a web page, a snippet of code that has to become a document someone can print or email. The converters people find either watermark the result, cap the free version at a few pages, demand an account before the first download, or quietly mangle the layout — and formatting loss is the single most complained-about problem with HTML-to-PDF conversion, documented in vendor troubleshooting guides for years: fonts change size, spacing collapses, tables break. On social media the same need shows up from the other side, with people asking how to do the reverse of a PDF-to-HTML tutorial — turn the HTML they already have back into a PDF — and getting told to just print the page from a browser. This page does that job directly: paste the HTML, check the preview, download the PDF, no account, no watermark.

How do I convert HTML to PDF for free?

Paste your HTML or upload a .html file, pick a page size, and download a PDF — free, no signup, no watermark. It keeps your text and basic layout by rendering the page in your browser first. It is not a full browser engine, so JavaScript, videos and fancy CSS may not come across.

An HTML to PDF converter is a tool that takes HTML — the code every web page is written in — and saves it as a PDF document. This one renders your HTML in a hidden frame in your browser, photographs that render page by page, and packs the images into a PDF with the jsPDF library. That is why text stays readable and the layout mostly survives, but also why the PDF is a picture of the page rather than selectable text.

When to use your browser's Print to PDF instead

If you need selectable text, clickable links, or a pixel-perfect copy of a live web page, use the browser: open the page, press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac), choose "Save as PDF". That uses the browser's own print engine and keeps text as text. Use this tool when you have raw HTML code or a file, when you want a quick PDF without opening a print dialog, or when you need the same result on any device without touching browser settings.

The Ctrl+P trick: a clean PDF in 10 seconds

The most-shared free method on social media is not a converter at all — it is your browser's print dialog. Open the page, press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac), set the destination to “Save as PDF”, then make two changes before you save: uncheck “Headers and footers” (which removes the page URL and date from every page) and turn off “Background graphics” (which removes background colours and images, stripping most ads and sidebars). What is left is the article or document, clean. This works on any page you can open — including pages behind a login, which no online converter can reach — and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

FAQ

Is this HTML to PDF converter really free?

Yes. Convert as many pages as you like, free, with no signup, no watermark and no page limit set by us.

Why does my PDF look different from the web page?

The PDF is a rendered image of your HTML, made in your browser. JavaScript does not run, some advanced CSS is approximated, and web fonts or external images that fail to load fall back to defaults.

Can I convert a website URL to PDF?

Not here — this tool converts HTML code or a file, not a web address. Save the page as HTML first (right-click, Save as), then upload the file.

Does the PDF keep clickable links?

No. The PDF is a rendered image of the page, so links appear as text but are not clickable. For clickable links, use your browser's Print to PDF.

Is my HTML uploaded to a server?

No. The HTML is rendered and converted entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or shared.

What page sizes are supported?

A4 and US Letter, in portrait or landscape. Long pages are split across as many PDF pages as needed.

How do I save a webpage as a clean PDF without ads?

Press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac), choose Save as PDF, then uncheck “Headers and footers” and turn off “Background graphics” — that strips sidebars, pop-ups and ads from the saved PDF.

Can I convert a login-protected page to PDF?

Usually not with an online converter — it can't log in as you. Use your browser's print-to-PDF while logged in, or a screenshot/PDF extension.

Why is my table split across two pages in the PDF?

Page-break rules are ignored inside flexbox/grid layouts and by some renderers. Add break-inside: avoid on tables in a @media print stylesheet, or pick a converter with smart page-break handling.

Can I convert HTML to PDF on my phone?

Yes — browser-based converters work on mobile, and on iPhone or Android you can also use the browser's Share, then Print, then Save as PDF flow. Step-by-step for every phone browser was not verified in our research.

Is it safe to upload my HTML to a free converter?

Risky for private content — invoices and internal pages go to the converter's server. Sejda auto-deletes files after 2 hours. For sensitive pages, use in-browser rendering or your own browser's print-to-PDF, where nothing is uploaded.

How do I convert multiple web pages to PDF at once?

Sejda accepts several URLs, one per line, in a single task. Batch conversion is otherwise usually a paid feature.

What is the difference between HTML to PDF and print to PDF?

Same idea: a converter renders your HTML or URL on its server with extra controls (page size, batch, headers); print-to-PDF renders the page exactly as your browser shows it, free, with no upload.

What is the best free HTML to PDF converter?

For occasional use, Sejda is the most-cited free option — URL, file and pasted code, page controls, no watermark within its free limits (3 tasks an hour, 30 MB). iLovePDF is the usual pick for URL-to-PDF with a live preview. This is roundup consensus, not a measured ranking.
