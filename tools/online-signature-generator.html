About This Tool

A signature is still the fastest way to make a document feel personal, even in a world of typed names and email approvals. This generator gives you a clean digital version of yours in seconds, without installing an app, creating an account, or handing your name to a server. Type your name and pick one of three elegant script fonts — Great Vibes for a formal flourish, Dancing Script for a friendly handwritten feel, or Sacramento for a light, casual stroke — or switch to the drawing pad and sign with your mouse, trackpad or finger, exactly as you would on paper. Either way, the result downloads as a transparent PNG, ready to drop into a document, a PDF, an email signature, or a website.

Transparency is what makes this tool genuinely useful rather than just decorative. A signature saved on a white background always leaves an ugly white rectangle when pasted onto a coloured letterhead, a photo, or a shaded form field. Here the canvas is never filled with a background colour, so the PNG carries a real alpha channel: only the ink itself is visible, and whatever sits underneath shows through. That is the same trick professional designers use for logos, and it means one download works everywhere — Word, Google Docs, PDF editors, Canva, email clients, and image editors — without any extra cleanup.

Because everything runs in your browser, your signature stays yours. Nothing you type or draw is uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere, and there is no account to create and no watermark stamped on the result. You can generate as many versions as you like — try a different font, a bolder size, blue ink instead of black — and keep the one that looks most like you. If you draw your signature, the undo button removes one stroke at a time, so a shaky line does not mean starting from scratch.

Where to use your digital signature

A transparent PNG signature is useful far beyond signing letters. Freelancers paste it into invoices and proposals to look established; job seekers add it to cover letters and CVs; small business owners put it on quotes, receipts, and certificates; teachers and coaches sign digital worksheets and completion certificates. It also works as a personal watermark on photos and artwork, and as the sign-off image in an email signature block. For any of these uses, keep the file at a generous size and shrink it in the document rather than stretching a small one — the download is exported at full canvas resolution, so it stays crisp when resized down.

One honest limit to keep in mind: a signature image is a picture, not a certified electronic signature. It is perfect for informal and everyday use, but contracts, legal filings, and regulated documents usually need a proper e-signature service that records who signed, when, and with what consent. Use this tool for the everyday cases, and reach for a dedicated e-signature platform when the document carries legal weight.

FAQ

Is this online signature generator really free?

Yes. Create and download unlimited signatures as transparent PNGs — no watermark, no sign-up, no usage limit. The generator runs entirely in your browser, so your name and signature never leave your device.

Will my signature have a transparent background?

Yes. The download is a real PNG with an alpha channel — the canvas is never filled with a background colour, so the signature drops cleanly onto documents, photos, PDFs and letterheads with no white box around it.

Is a signature made here legally valid?

A typed or drawn signature image is a picture of a signature, not a certified electronic signature. It is fine for informal use such as emails, documents, watermarks and branding, but for contracts and legally binding documents use a proper e-signature service that records consent, identity and an audit trail.

What if the script fonts do not load?

The page loads Great Vibes, Dancing Script and Sacramento from Google Fonts. If those fonts are blocked — for example on an offline connection — your name still renders in your browser's built-in cursive fallback font, and the drawing pad always works regardless of fonts.

Can I use my signature on a document or email?

Yes. Download the transparent PNG and insert it into Word, Google Docs, a PDF editor or your email signature settings, then resize it to fit the signature line. Because the background is transparent it blends into any page colour.
