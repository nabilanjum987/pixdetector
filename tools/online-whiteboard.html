About This Tool

A whiteboard is the fastest way to think out loud. Whether you are sketching a website layout, working through a maths problem with a student, mapping a business idea, or just doodling while an idea forms, a blank canvas with a pen beats a blank document every time. This free online whiteboard gives you that canvas instantly — no account, no app to install, no meeting link to join. Open the page and start drawing with your mouse, trackpad, finger or stylus.

Everything happens in your browser. Your strokes are never uploaded to a server, stored in an account, or shared with anyone else, so you can sketch client work, homework answers, passwords-as-diagrams or anything else without worrying about who else can see it. The board is yours alone: when you close the tab it is gone, which is exactly why the one-click PNG export is there — save the board whenever a sketch is worth keeping, and it downloads as a standard image file you can paste into a document, send in a message, or print.

The tool is deliberately simple, because a whiteboard should stay out of your way. Eight pen colours cover the usual jobs — black for writing, red for corrections, blue and green for diagrams, and the rest for highlighting — plus a custom colour picker for anything else. The brush slider runs from a fine 1-pixel line for writing and detail work up to a thick 40-pixel marker for headings and emphasis. The eraser rubs out mistakes without touching the rest of your drawing, and undo steps back through your strokes one at a time, so a slip of the hand costs one click instead of the whole board.

How to use the whiteboard in three steps

STEP 1

Pick a pen and draw

Choose a colour from the palette, set your brush size, then press and drag on the canvas. It works with a mouse, trackpad, finger or stylus.

STEP 2

Fix mistakes as you go

Use the eraser to rub out a section, or Undo to step back through your strokes one at a time. Clear wipes the whole board when you want a fresh start.

STEP 3

Save your board

Click Save as PNG to download your drawing as a standard image file — share it, print it, or paste it into a document.

What is an online whiteboard good for?

Teachers use it to explain a problem live on a video call, tutors to work through homework with a student, and parents to practise handwriting or drawing with children. Freelancers sketch wireframes and logo ideas before opening design software, and small teams map out a process or brainstorm on a call without paying for a collaboration suite. It is also just a pleasant place to doodle: a blank page with no rules and no audience. Because the board is private by default, it suits anything you would not want on a shared or recorded board — rough drafts, half-formed ideas, and notes that are only for you. If you need to keep a result, export the PNG before you close the tab; if you only needed to think something through, close it and the board disappears with it.

FAQ

Is this online whiteboard really free?

Yes. The whiteboard is completely free to use with no time limit, no watermark and no sign-up. Draw as much as you like and save your board as a PNG as often as you want.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. The whiteboard runs entirely in your browser — there is no account to create, no app to install and no extension to add. Open the page and start drawing straight away.

Can I save my whiteboard drawing?

Yes. Click 'Save as PNG' and your whole board downloads as a standard PNG image file you can share, print, paste into a document or keep as a record of your session.

Does the whiteboard work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. The board supports mouse, trackpad, finger and stylus input, so you can sketch on a phone or tablet just as easily as on a desktop. On touch screens, use one finger to draw and two fingers to scroll the page.

Is anything I draw uploaded or saved online?

No. Everything you draw stays on your own device — nothing is uploaded to a server, stored in an account or shared with anyone. Closing the tab erases the board, so save a PNG first if you want to keep it.
