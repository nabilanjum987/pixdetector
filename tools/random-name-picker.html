The problem: calling on the same few people every time

Every teacher knows it: ask a question and the same three hands go up, while the rest of the class quietly checks out. Every giveaway host knows the other version: draw a winner by hand and someone in the comments says it was fixed. The complaints about name pickers follow the same pattern as the wheel — tools that ship with fake sample names you have to delete first, tools that pick the same person twice in a row with no way to stop it, and tools that make you create an account before you can use your own class list. What teachers and hosts actually want is the simplest possible thing: paste my list, pick one fairly, and do not pick the same person twice until everyone has had a turn.

How do I pick a random name from a list?

Paste your names below, one per line, choose how many to pick, and press PICK A NAME — free, with no signup, and the pick happens instantly in your browser. With "No repeats" turned on, each name sits out until the whole list has had a turn, then everyone is back in the pool. Every name in your list has exactly the same chance of being picked.

A random name picker is a tool that chooses one or more names from a list you provide. This one has no built-in names — the list comes from you, whether that is a class register, a team roster, or a list of giveaway entrants — and the pick is made with your browser's secure random generator, so the result cannot be steered.

How to pick a random name in 3 steps

Step 1: paste your names, one per line — copy them straight from a spreadsheet or a document. Step 2: choose how many names to pick (1 to 10) and leave "No repeats" on if nobody should be picked twice. Step 3: press PICK A NAME. When everyone has had a turn, press "Start a new round" to put all names back in.

Fair picks for classrooms

The "No repeats" mode is built for classrooms: it works like drawing names from a hat, one at a time, without putting them back. Nobody is called on twice while someone else has never been called on, which keeps the whole class paying attention — anyone could be next. When the round is over, one button resets the pool. If you want a visible draw the class can watch, the spin the wheel tool picks from the same kind of list with an animated wheel instead.

What this picker cannot do

It cannot invent names for you. If you need name ideas — for a character, a baby, a team or a business — that is a different tool: a name generator creates names, while this picker only chooses from names you already have. It also cannot save your list between visits: your names stay in this browser tab and are gone when you close the page, which is also why nothing you paste is ever uploaded or seen by anyone else.

FAQ

Is this random name picker really free?

Yes. Pick as many names as you like with no signup, no payment and no download. Everything runs in your browser.

Does this tool have built-in names?

No. The list comes from you — paste your own names, one per line. This picker chooses from your list; it does not invent names.

How do I pick a random student for my class?

Paste your class list, one name per line, leave “No repeats” on, and press PICK A NAME. Each student sits out until the whole class has had a turn.

Can I pick more than one name at a time?

Yes — choose how many to pick, from 1 to 10. With “No repeats” on, all the picked names are different.

Can the same name be picked twice?

Only if you turn “No repeats” off. With it on, a picked name stays out of the pool until you start a new round.

Is my list of names uploaded or saved?

No. Your names stay in your browser tab and are never sent to a server, stored, or shared. Close the page and they are gone.
