What this tool does — and what it does not do

What it does: a DynamicsCompressor used as a soft noise gate pushes quiet background noise down between words, a high-pass filter cuts low rumble, and EQ shapes the voice — all in your browser, with an A/B comparison before you download, and no sign-in required to get the file.

What it does not do: it cannot remove irregular sounds (coughs, traffic, barking, other people talking), cannot repair clipped or distorted audio, cannot restore detail lost in a low-bitrate recording, and cannot match AI tools on heavy or complex noise. Push it too hard and voices sound processed or metallic — that is the cue to use gentler settings. These filters are tuned for voice, not music: steady background noise only; voices and sudden sounds stay.

How to get the cleanest result

Find the quietest part of your recording and set the gate just above its level — around −40 dB works for most rooms. If quiet words start getting chopped, raise the threshold (towards −30 dB) or lower the gate strength. Keep the rumble cut at 80 Hz for voice; higher cuts thin the voice. Add a touch of mid EQ (+2 dB) for presence, but keep high EQ modest (+3 dB) or hiss comes back. Record better next time if you can: move closer to the mic, turn the fan off for the take, and record a few seconds of room tone first — that silence is what a gate needs to tell noise from voice.

FAQ

Is this background noise remover really free?

Yes. Noise removal is free with no sign-up, no upload, no watermark and no cap — and unlike some tools, there is no sign-in required to download the cleaned file.

What kind of noise can it remove?

Steady background noise: fan hum, air-conditioning, hiss and room tone. The noise gate pushes quiet, constant noise down between words, and the high-pass filter cuts low rumble.

Why can't it remove traffic, coughs or other voices?

Filters can only tell noise from voice when the noise is steady and quieter than the voice. Traffic, coughs, barking and other people talking are sudden or voice-like, so a filter cannot separate them — AI tools do better here, with an account and free-tier limits.

Why does the cleaned voice sound metallic?

Over-cleaning. Too strong a gate or too much high EQ strips the natural room sound and leaves the voice processed. Re-enhance with gentler settings — raise the gate threshold towards −30 dB and lower the high EQ — and compare again.

Do I need to sign in to download the cleaned audio?

No. The cleaned WAV downloads directly, with no account and no one-file-a-day limit. Everything runs in your browser.

Does my audio get uploaded to a server?
