A world clock shows the current local time in cities around the world at the same moment. There are not 24 time zones — there are about 38–40, counting half-hour and 45-minute offsets such as India at UTC+5:30, Nepal at UTC+5:45 and the Chatham Islands at UTC+12:45, spanning UTC−12 to UTC+14. Every zone is an offset from UTC — Coordinated Universal Time, the world's time standard. A city ahead of UTC, like Tokyo at UTC+9, is nine hours ahead of London in winter; a city behind it, like New York at UTC-5, is five hours behind.

How time zones actually work

Every place on Earth keeps time as an offset from UTC. London sits at UTC+0 in winter and UTC+1 in summer (British Summer Time). New York is UTC-5 in winter and UTC-4 in summer. Tokyo never changes — UTC+9 all year. That is why the gap between two cities changes through the year: New York and London are usually 5 hours apart, but only 4 in the gap weeks when one side has changed its clocks and the other has not — late October, when Europe changes first, and March, when the US changes first. This page never does that maths by hand — it asks your browser for each city's real offset right now, including daylight saving, so the times are right on any date.

The rule of thumb: a 9-to-5 overlap only exists when two cities are within about 8 hours of each other. Beyond that, one side is always outside working hours — pick a time that is merely inconvenient for both, not impossible for one.

“3 PM whose time?” — the one rule that stops missed meetings

Never send a bare time. Always name the zone — “3 PM London time”, not “3 PM” — and send a link or a converted time instead of asking the other person to do the maths in their head. Use “ET” or “Eastern Time”, not “EST”: writing EST in summer is an hour wrong, because EST is the winter abbreviation (UTC−5) and summer is EDT (UTC−4). For a recurring meeting, say which city’s clock the series follows — a meeting that stays at the same local time in its home zone silently moves for everyone else when the clocks change. The converter above does all three: it names both zones, it works on a future date (so the DST rules for that date apply), and it gives you a shareable link and a copy-for-Slack line.

FAQ

What time is it in London, New York or Tokyo right now?

The live clock for each city is at the top of this page — London, New York and Tokyo are on the board by default, with the date and UTC offset shown on every card. Add any other city from the search box.

How many time zones are there?

More than 24 — about 38–40 counting half-hour and 45-minute offsets such as India at UTC+5:30, Nepal at UTC+5:45 and the Chatham Islands at UTC+12:45, spanning UTC−12 to UTC+14. “24” is the common wrong answer.

What is the time difference between New York and London?

Usually 5 hours, with New York behind London — but 4 hours in the week between Europe ending daylight saving on 25 Oct 2026 and the US ending it on 1 Nov 2026. Never quote a fixed gap without the date.

Is it day or night in a city right now?

Each card on this page shows a day-part label — Morning, Work hours, Evening or Asleep — next to the live time, so you can tell at a glance whether someone is awake. It is a time-of-day cue, not a sunrise or sunset forecast.

Does a place observe daylight saving time?

Many places do not — Japan, South Korea, India, most of Arizona and Hawaii among them — and their gap to places that do observe it changes twice a year.

When do the clocks change in 2026?

In Europe the clocks went back on 25 Oct 2026; in the US they go back on 1 Nov 2026. The two changes a week apart are what create the gap week, when London–New York is 4 hours instead of the usual 5.

What is the difference between EST and EDT?

EST is Eastern Standard Time (UTC−5, winter); EDT is Eastern Daylight Time (UTC−4, summer). Writing “EST” in summer is an hour wrong — “ET” or “Eastern Time” is the safe form.

How do I schedule a meeting across time zones?

Pick the cities, look at the meeting overlap finder on this page for a slot inside everyone’s 9-to-5, and send the invite with the time zone named — never a bare time.

Why did my recurring meeting move an hour?

It did not move in its home zone — the gap between the zones changed when one side’s clocks changed. Check which city’s clock the series follows.

What does UTC+5:30 mean?

That place is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). Half-hour offsets are real — India is UTC+5:30, not +5 or +6.

What time is it in UTC / GMT now?

UTC is the reference all zones are measured against; GMT is effectively the same clock reading for everyday use. The live UTC clock is shown at the top of this page.

Can I see the time in multiple cities at once?

Yes — add each city as a card on one board; this page does it free with no account.

Is the world clock accurate?

Does the world clock work offline?

Yes — after the page loads, the clocks tick from your device clock, so they keep working offline. Your saved cities are stored in this browser.
