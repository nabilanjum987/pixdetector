A YouTube thumbnail is the 1280×720 still image that represents your video in search results, on the home feed and in "Up next". YouTube recommends 1280×720 pixels (16:9, at least 640 wide), under 2 MB. It is the single biggest lever on click-through rate after the title itself — a custom thumbnail beats YouTube's auto-generated frame almost every time, because the auto frame is usually a random mid-sentence face.

How to make a YouTube thumbnail in 4 steps

Step 1: pick a design — Bold gradient for text-led videos, Photo + text when you have a strong face or shot, Split colour for a clean two-tone look. YouTube thumbnail size and rules

Size: 1280×720 pixels, 16:9, at least 640 pixels wide. File: JPG, PNG or GIF, up to 2 MB — a PNG from this maker is usually well under that, but a very detailed photo background can push it close; if so, the file still downloads fine and YouTube accepts it up to the limit. Overlays: the video length covers the bottom-right corner, and a red progress bar crosses the bottom for videos the viewer has already watched. Custom thumbnails need a verified channel in YouTube Studio, and you can change a thumbnail any time after upload.

What size should a YouTube thumbnail be?

1280 by 720 pixels, 16:9, under 2 MB, in JPG, PNG or GIF. This tool exports exactly 1280 by 720 PNG, which YouTube accepts. Custom thumbnails need a verified channel in YouTube Studio.

How many words should I put on a thumbnail?

Three to five words, not ten. The video title already sits under the thumbnail, so the thumbnail only needs the hook — in letters big enough to read on a phone. Use the small-size preview on this page to check yours.

Will my thumbnail be readable on a phone?

That is the real test: most viewers see your thumbnail tiny, in a feed. Use the phone-size preview toggle here — if the words still read at that size, the thumbnail works. Thick outlines and strong contrast (light text on dark, or the reverse) are what save text on busy photos.

Related questions

How do I change a thumbnail on YouTube? In YouTube Studio, open Content, click the pencil on a video, and under Thumbnail choose Upload thumbnail. It works on videos that have been live for years.

What fonts work best for thumbnails? Bold, heavy fonts that stay readable tiny — the thick style used here. Avoid thin or script fonts; they turn illegible at feed size.

Can I make thumbnails on my phone? Yes — this page works in a mobile browser. Upload a photo, type the words, download the PNG, then upload it in the YouTube Studio app.

Do I need Photoshop to make a good thumbnail? No. A clean 1280×720 image with three big words and strong contrast beats a complicated Photoshop design that nobody can read at phone size.
